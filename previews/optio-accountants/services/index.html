Services

Practical compliance and advisory for businesses and individuals across Tarragindi and Brisbane South, QLD. Packages are scoped after a short discovery call.

01

Individual & company tax returns

Accurate lodgement for sole traders, companies, trusts and individuals around Tarragindi, with plain-English summaries of what you owe and why.

  • Personal returns for employees, contractors and investors
  • Company and trust returns with distribution statements

BAS, GST & payroll

Quarterly BAS, STP payroll and super reconciliations so cash flow and compliance stay aligned through the year.

Includes

  • GST coding reviews and BAS lodgement

Bookkeeping & cloud setup

Business advisory
